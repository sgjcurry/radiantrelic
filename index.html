Course Overview

This course introduces the principles and practical techniques behind nuclear physics instrumentation. Students will explore detector systems, electronics, data acquisition, signal processing, and experimental analysis used in modern research.

What You Will Learn

Radiation Detectors

Silicon detectors, scintillators, HPGe systems and more.

Signal Processing

Pulse shaping, timing electronics and spectroscopy.

Data Acquisition

DAQ systems, triggering and experimental control.

Data Analysis

ROOT, Python, calibration techniques and uncertainty analysis.

  3. Detector Technologies

  6. Data Acquisition Systems

  8. Experimental Data Analysis
  9. Current Research Applications
