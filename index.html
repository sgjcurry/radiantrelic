Detectors and Sources in Nuclear Physics Course

Learn about modern nuclear physics experiments using radioactive ion beams, such as those at CERN's ISOLDE, and how they detect particles and gamma-rays using different instrumentation and analysis methods.

Contact to enroll

Course Overview

This course introduces the principles and techniques behind nuclear physics experiments, namely using radioactive ion beams to study gamma-ray spectroscopy of exotic nuclei. Students will explore detector systems, electronics, data acquisition, signal processing, and experimental analysis used in modern research.

What You Will Learn

Radiation Detectors

Silicon and HPGe detectors and their processing components.

Exotic Radioactive Nuclei

Production and safety of radioactie ion beams.

Signal Processing

Pulse shaping, timing electronics and gamma-gamma spectroscopy.

Data Acquisition

DAQ systems, triggering and experimental control.

Data Analysis

ROOT, Python, calibration techniques and uncertainty analysis.

Course Modules

  1. Introduction to modern Nuclear Physics Experiments
  2. Radiation Interactions with Matter
  3. Detector Technologies
  4. Radioactive Ion Beam production and safety
  5. Signal Processing and DAQs
  6. Experimental Data Analysis
  7. Current and future Research Applications
